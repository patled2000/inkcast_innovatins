Inkcast Innovations

What began as a passion project in 2020 has now evolved into a creative movement that blends art, emotion, and strategy to build unforgettable brand experiences. Initially thriving as a freelance graphic design venture, Inkcast quickly gained trust and admiration from clients across diverse industries — from home bakers to hotels, educational institutes to IT firms.
As the demand for meaningful, result-driven design grew, so did we. In March 2024, we expanded our team, our vision, and our services to step into the market as Inkcast Innovations, based in the vibrant heart of Indore.
Today, we offer end-to-end digital marketing solutions that include not only stunning graphic design but also social media management, Facebook & Google ads, SEO, print media, and video production. Our team of creative strategists, designers, marketers, and storytellers work together to craft visuals that do more than just look good — they deliver measurable impact.

Why Us

You want results. We have found that the best way to get them is through up-front research of the market, competitors, target audience, and customer psychographics. Only after we fully understand you and your needs, we recommend a plan of attack.

We bring our diverse team of experts from advertising, design, production, public relations, research, and strategic planning to work for your brand. Not only the approach — the results also look great.
